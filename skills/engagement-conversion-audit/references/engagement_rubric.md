# On-Site Engagement & Conversion Rubric for AI Citation Landings

## The AI Referral Visitor Mindset
Visitors who click an AI citation (from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Gemini) have specific, high-intent expectations:
1. **Context Alignment**: The user expects to immediately see the exact fact, feature, or pricing quoted by the AI model.
2. **Immediate Orientation**: Because AI assistants often link directly to deep subpages (e.g. `/features/security-audit` or `/pricing#enterprise`), the user did not experience the brand's homepage narrative.
3. **Zero Tolerance for Friction**: Intrusive modals, full-screen email capture popups, or confusing site navigation result in immediate bounce back to the AI assistant.

## Audit Dimensions
- **Dimension 1: Deep Landing Orientation**: Presence of persistent breadcrumbs, parent brand hierarchy, and site taxonomy.
- **Dimension 2: Above-the-Fold Value Proposition**: Clear headline and 1-2 sentence explanation visible immediately without scrolling.
- **Dimension 3: Friction & Interruption**: Absence of obstructive blocking modals or layout shifts.
- **Dimension 4: Next-Step CTA Pathways**: Prominent primary action button and secondary exploratory pathway.
